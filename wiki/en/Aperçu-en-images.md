# Screenshots

The main screens of the map, taken on a real network (Haute-Loire, France, November 2026) in demo mode. The interface is available in 9 languages.

## The live map
Meshtastic and MeshCore relays, gateways and nodes, radio links coloured by SNR, dashboard and legend.

![The live map](images/apercu/en/01-carte.jpg)

## Relay sheet
Signal, hops, hardware, role, and actions: coverage, range test, remote programming, settings QR code.

![Relay sheet](images/apercu/en/02-fiche-relais.jpg)

## Telemetry and health
Uptime, restarts, daily voltage over 7 days and latest traceroutes.

![Telemetry and health](images/apercu/en/03-telemetrie.jpg)

## Coverage from a point on real terrain
Where a relay placed here would be heard, on real terrain, with the area covered.

![Coverage from a point on real terrain](images/apercu/en/04-couverture-relais.jpg)

## Whole network coverage
Area covered by the detected relays and what each one adds (a relay that adds nothing stands out).

![Whole network coverage](images/apercu/en/05-couverture-reseau.jpg)

## Terrain profile and link budget
Line of sight, Fresnel zone, losses, link margin between two points.

![Terrain profile and link budget](images/apercu/en/06-profil.jpg)

## Choosing where to put a relay
Coverage plan for the area, best high points, and a reminder: a high point is not an authorised site.

![Choosing where to put a relay](images/apercu/en/07-sites.jpg)

## Node list
Meshtastic and MeshCore in one list, by type, with hops and last heard.

![Node list](images/apercu/en/08-noeuds.jpg)

## Live radio traffic
Packets filtered by type: position, telemetry, text, node info, traceroute…

![Live radio traffic](images/apercu/en/09-trafic.jpg)

## Statistics
Channel utilisation, packets, overly chatty nodes and settings to review.

![Statistics](images/apercu/en/10-statistiques.jpg)

## MeshCore
Repeaters, room servers, companions, packets, analysis and MeshCore messages.

![MeshCore](images/apercu/en/11-meshcore.jpg)

## Remote administration
Program Meshtastic nodes over LoRa: admin keys, templates, favourites, firmware, teams.

![Remote administration](images/apercu/en/12-gerer.jpg)

## 3D view on aerial photos
The network on the terrain, links of the last 24 h, rotate as you like.

![3D view on aerial photos](images/apercu/en/13-vue-3d-photos.jpg)

## 3D view, dark base map
Exaggerated terrain to see valleys and ridges better.

![3D view, dark base map](images/apercu/en/14-vue-3d-sombre.jpg)

## On a phone
The map also works on a phone and installs like an app.

![On a phone](images/apercu/en/15-telephone.jpg)

## Mesh topology
Link graph, distance to gateways, breaking points and critical links.

![Mesh topology](images/apercu/en/16-topologie.jpg)

## Relay register
Site, permission, last visit, power and firmware of every relay, exportable as CSV.

![Relay register](images/apercu/en/17-registre.jpg)
