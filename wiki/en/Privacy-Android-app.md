# Privacy policy — "Carte live" Android app

*Last updated: 18 November 2026 · Publisher: Olivier Parriaux (F4GLD), open-source project [carte-live-meshtastic](https://github.com/sauveteur71/carte-live-meshtastic)*

The "Carte live" app is a window onto a **self-hosted** live map: on first launch you choose the address of your network's server (club, association, personal install). The app's publisher runs **no server** that would receive your data.

## Data collected by the publisher

**None.** No ads, no analytics, no trackers, no account with the publisher. No data is sold or shared with the publisher.

## Data exchanged with the map you choose

The app shows **your** server's map. What you do there (signing in, messages, reports, position) goes to that server, run by its administrator, who is responsible for that data.

- **Location (GPS)**: used only when you turn it on in the map (show your position, report a point, guidance to a beacon). Sent to the server only when you ask (report, position sharing). No background tracking.
- **Notifications**: if you enable the watch service, the app polls the chosen map every 45 s (with your session) to show direct messages, critical alerts and reports. You can stop it at any time in the app settings.
- **Local network**: map discovery only scans your local Wi-Fi network for a map server; nothing is sent elsewhere.

## Data kept on the phone

Your list of maps, the last map opened and the app settings stay on the phone, and are erased when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

## Security

Prefer an **https://** address for your map so traffic is encrypted. On a local network, http:// remains possible.

## Children

The app is meant for radio amateurs, rescuers and radio network users; it is not directed at children.
